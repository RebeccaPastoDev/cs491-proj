# Rover Design

## Overview

The team is developing a lunar rover as part of a Lunar Digital Twin project using Unity and Virtual Reality (VR).

## Rover Components

The rover consists of a main body, four wheels, and solar panels. These components are represented in the Unity environment.

## Movement and Navigation

The rover is designed to move across the simulated lunar surface. The team is working on wheel movement, traction, rover physics, and navigation.

## Lunar Environment Interaction

The rover must interact with the lunar terrain and avoid obstacles such as rocks. Collision detection is used to prevent the rover from passing through the terrain or environmental objects.

## Development Goals

- Develop a functional rover model.
- Simulate realistic rover movement.
- Improve interaction with lunar terrain.
- Integrate the rover into the virtual lunar environment.
