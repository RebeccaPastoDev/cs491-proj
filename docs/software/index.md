# 💻 Rutgers University – Software Development and Digital Twin

## 🔵 Introduction

The Rutgers University team was responsible for developing the software and virtual components of the NASA Lunar Rover Digital Twin project. Our work focused on creating a virtual lunar environment and developing the software systems needed to represent, monitor, and interact with the rover.

The goal was to bring together software development, digital twin technology, autonomous navigation, and Virtual Reality (VR) to create an interactive lunar exploration experience.

## ⚙️ Our Work and Contributions

Throughout the project, the Rutgers team worked on several important areas:

- 🌐 **Virtual Lunar Environment:** Developed a lunar surface environment in Unity using Cesium, adding terrain, rocks, stations, solar panels, and other environmental elements to create a more realistic exploration setting.

- 💻 **Digital Twin Development:** Worked on the virtual representation of the rover and the connection between the physical rover and its digital counterpart.

- 🧭 **Rover Navigation:** Worked on pathfinding functionality using the A* algorithm to help the virtual rover identify routes and navigate through the lunar environment.

- 📊 **Rover Monitoring and Interface:** Developed user interface features to display rover information, monitor its status, and communicate important system information.

- 🥽 **Virtual Reality Development:** Worked on creating an immersive VR experience that allows users to explore and interact with the virtual lunar environment.

## 🤝 Team Collaboration

The Rutgers team worked collaboratively, with members contributing to different software components and development tasks. Through teamwork, testing, and integration, we brought together the virtual environment, rover systems, navigation features, and user interface into a shared project.

Each contribution helped move the project closer to its overall goal of creating an interactive digital twin of a lunar rover.

## 🏆 Rutgers Team Accomplishments

Our work represents an important part of the overall project. We developed the foundation of the virtual lunar environment, implemented software features, and worked toward connecting the physical rover with its digital representation.

These accomplishments demonstrate our progress in software engineering, problem-solving, system integration, and collaborative development.

The result is a virtual platform designed to support lunar rover exploration and provide an immersive experience through digital twin and VR technology.
